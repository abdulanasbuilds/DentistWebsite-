How often should I visit the dentist for checkups?

Most people benefit from a routine dental checkup and cleaning every six months. Your dentist may recommend more frequent visits based on your individual oral-health needs.

What can I do to prevent tooth decay effectively?

Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste, clean between your teeth every day, limit sugary snacks and drinks, and keep up with regular dental checkups.

Are professional teeth cleanings necessary for oral health?

Yes. Professional cleanings remove hardened plaque and tartar that brushing alone cannot reach, helping lower the risk of cavities, gum disease, and bad breath.

What should I expect during my first dental appointment?

Your first visit usually includes a review of your health history, an examination of your teeth and gums, possible X-rays, and time to discuss any concerns or treatment options.

How can I keep my gums healthy every day?

Brush gently along the gumline twice a day, floss or use interdental cleaners daily, eat a balanced diet, and book a dental visit if you notice bleeding, swelling, or sensitivity.
